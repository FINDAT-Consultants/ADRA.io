ASSURANCE REGENT v6.3.14 — VACANCY SOCIAL SHARING

WHAT CHANGED
- Every externally published vacancy has a Share control in HR Recruiting.
- HR can share to Facebook, WhatsApp, X/Twitter, LinkedIn, the device native share sheet, copy the exact vacancy URL, or copy a ready-to-post vacancy message.
- Shared links point only to the isolated public careers page and open the exact vacancy.
- Public vacancy cards and the public job-details view also include a Share action.
- No HR session, employee, applicant or internal-system information is placed in the share URL.

DATABASE
No new SQL migration is required for v6.3.14. Continue using the corrected v6.3.13 HOTFIX 1 database.

DEPLOYMENT
Deploy the /public directory from this package. No Edge Function redeployment is required solely for vacancy sharing.
