Sign Online Memorandum of Understanding ⌄

Online Memorandum of Understanding Summary

This online MOU confirms that the company wishes to partner with ADRA Uganda to provide safe, dignified, and supervised employment or internship opportunities for eligible girls and young women supported by ADRA.

  1. The company agrees to provide a safe, respectful, non-discriminatory working environment.
  2. ADRA will recommend potential candidates based on education, skills, performance, and readiness.
  3. The company will interview, select, supervise, and provide feedback on recruited candidates.
  4. Both parties will protect beneficiary privacy and use data only for recruitment, support, monitoring, and reporting.
  5. The company will update ADRA on recruitment outcomes, internship progress, and job placement status.
