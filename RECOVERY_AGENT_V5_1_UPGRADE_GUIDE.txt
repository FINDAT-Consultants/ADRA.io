EXPECTED BEHAVIOR
- On Dashboard, Company, Payroll, Projects and other permitted pages: floating Recovery AI Operator appears.
- On Recovery Agent page: floating Recovery AI Operator and launcher are hidden.
- Clear conversation on the Recovery Agent page clears the floating operator conversation too.
- Clear in the floating operator clears the dedicated Recovery Agent page conversation too.
- The floating operator has a visible Clear control.
- Voice button toggles between mute and unmute. Muting pauses current speech; unmuting can resume it.
- Microphone button becomes Stop recording while recording.
