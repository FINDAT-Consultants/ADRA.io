ASSURANCE REGENT — JIVAN v5.12.0 DEPLOYMENT GUIDE

PURPOSE
v5.12.0 removes Wake Mode and replaces it with a user-started, continuous turn-taking voice conversation.

VOICE FLOW
1. Press Voice conversation once.
2. The microphone session remains available while the mode is active.
3. Jivan waits for voice activity and starts a MediaRecorder turn only after speech is detected.
4. When the speaker pauses for about one second, the turn is automatically ended.
5. The audio is transcribed and submitted directly to Jivan. It is not placed in the text box waiting for Send.
6. Jivan speaks the reply using the existing locked human voice.
7. Microphone monitoring is paused during Jivan speech to prevent self-transcription.
8. When Jivan finishes, the system automatically listens for the next user turn.
9. Press End voice to stop the session.

DEPLOYMENT
- No new Supabase SQL migration is required.
- No recovery-agent Edge Function replacement is required.
- Deploy the full v5.12.0 ZIP to the GitHub/Netlify project.
- Hard-refresh the deployed HTTPS site after Netlify publishes it.

BROWSER PERMISSION
The browser still owns first-use microphone permission. The application cannot bypass a browser denial. After permission is granted, the same microphone stream is reused during the active conversation session.

TEST
- Sign in.
- Open Jivan or the floating operator.
- Press Voice conversation once.
- Speak: "Open the dashboard and tell me what needs attention."
- Stop speaking and do not press Send.
- Jivan should automatically submit the turn and speak the response.
- When Jivan finishes, say another instruction without clicking Voice again.
- Use the Stop Speaking button to interrupt Jivan; listening should resume.
- Press End voice when finished.
