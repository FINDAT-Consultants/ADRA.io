ASSURANCE REGENT v6.3.16 — COMPANY-SPECIFIC GOOGLE MEET INTERVIEW ROOMS

EXISTING v6.3.15 / v6.3.13-HOTFIX DATABASE
1. Supabase SQL Editor: run COMPANY_INTERVIEW_ROOMS_V6_3_16.sql.
2. Run COMPANY_INTERVIEW_ROOMS_V6_3_16_VERIFY.sql.
3. Redeploy supabase/functions/recruitment-public/index.ts.
4. Deploy the updated /public folder.
5. Sign in as Developer. For every company, open Settings > Access, approvals & company directory and set a UNIQUE Google Meet interview room.

NEW COMPANY CREATION
The Developer must enter a Google Meet URL while creating the company. Assurance Regent stores it as interviewMeetUrl on that company. The URL must use https://meet.google.com/... and cannot be assigned to another company.

AUTOMATIC ROUTING
HR scheduling resolves the room from the application company. The interview record stores that company room. The HR Interview card and the public candidate interview card both use the stored company-specific room automatically.

NO GLOBAL FALLBACK
The old shared syq-rsnj-cwi room is removed as a fallback. Legacy interview rows are remapped to the owning company room when configured; otherwise their room is blank until Developer setup and rescheduling.
