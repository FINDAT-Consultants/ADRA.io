CONNECTED-AGENT BEHAVIOR
- Zari is the sign-in/sign-up receptionist and voice-authentication operator.
- The authentication header visibly shows Zari ↔ Jivan.
- After password or verified-voice sign-in, Zari says that access is confirmed and hands the user over to Jivan.
- Inside the signed-in system, a request beginning with “Zari” or asking to speak to Zari is answered as Zari through the same governed recovery-agent Edge Function.
- “Jivan, consult Zari …” runs a separate Zari consultation first, then Jivan receives that consultation and produces the final response. The UI may show the Zari consultation separately.
- Both agents remain inside identical role, tenant, privacy, approval and security boundaries.

VOICE LISTENING
- Access instructions no longer stop after about five seconds.
- Recording begins after the user clicks the microphone.
- It waits for actual speech, continues through natural speech, and closes after approximately 1.8 seconds of post-speech silence.
- No-speech timeout: approximately 15 seconds.
- Maximum instruction safety cap: 55 seconds.
- Voice recognition/enrollment also use end-of-speech detection with shorter biometric safety limits.
- Spoken passwords remain prohibited.
